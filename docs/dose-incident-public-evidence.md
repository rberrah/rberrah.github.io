Design: hypothetical normalized amount, D/V=1, F=1, exact linear steady-state oral superposition. Actual and population half-lives independent. Complete omission d=τ adds the modeled correction to the scheduled input, whereas a delay before τ replaces only the missed input. Vomiting loss applies to the whole depot present at the chosen event, including any previous-dose residual. Fractional loss allows sensitivity analysis; true loss is unknown. Show no correction and comparison strategies, never recommend a dose. Compare the first two scheduled intervals relative to the reference, not the original study's patient targets. AUC trapezoid on <=0.025h grid, sampled peak approximate. No clinical thresholds or real drug parameters as presets. Public scientific equation is verified; software and prospective clinical validation are separate, still absent.
