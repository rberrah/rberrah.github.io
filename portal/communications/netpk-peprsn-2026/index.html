NetPK: fusing published biological knowledge into pharmacokinetic models through network diffusion

A literature-augmented population PK framework — tacrolimus as proof of concept, built to generalize

Racym Berrah, Pierre Marquet, Jean-Baptiste Woillard

Event: PEPR Santé Numérique — Journées annuelles 2026 ↗

13 October 2026 – 15 October 2026 · Marseille, France

Site summary

NetPK encodes published tacrolimus pharmacology as network-prioritised, literature-fixed covariate modules in a nonlinear mixed-effects model. The poster reports an external sparse-sampling evaluation and virtual-patient use case, while identifying module ablation, uncertainty propagation and prospective dose-adjustment validation as next steps.
