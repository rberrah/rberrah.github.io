Toward autonomous pharmacometrics: a multi agent framework for structure discovery, covariate selection and gray-box modeling

Racym Berrah, Jean-Baptiste Woillard

Event: PAGE 2026 ↗

2 June 2026 – 5 June 2026 · Dubrovnik, Croatia

Site summary

This proof of concept combines language-model agents with a custom SAEM engine for iterative population-PK structure and covariate exploration. Simulated and clinical tacrolimus examples showed feasibility, while code instability, execution safeguards, computational cost and continued pharmacometric supervision remained explicit limitations.
