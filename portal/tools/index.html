Statistics

Stats

Research / educational software

Guided statistical testing with explicit rules and plain-language interpretation.

Stats starts from the scientific question and study design rather than requiring users to know the test name. The browser proposes a compatible method using deterministic rules, computes the analysis locally, and reports effect estimates, confidence intervals, p-values, effect sizes, assumptions and limitations.

  • Independent and paired quantitative comparisons.
  • Three or more independent groups.
  • Contingency tables and proportions.
  • Pearson / Spearman correlation and simple linear regression.
  • Built-in demonstrations, contextual help and French / English interface.

Browser-only research and educational calculator. High-stakes analyses and small-sample rank tests should be verified with reference statistical software.

Technical evidence: The repository documents supported statistical designs, deterministic selection rules and test limitations, enabling the methods to be inspected rather than inferred from the interface.

Omics analysis

Multiomics

Research / educational software

Analyze one omics layer or integrate transcriptomics, proteomics and metabolomics.

The deterministic tool supports exploratory PCA, group comparisons, longitudinal studies and outcomes when the study design permits. It offers RNA-only, proteomics-only and metabolomics-only demonstrations, sample sheet guidance, QC audits, biological pathway views and traceable MS peak-area imports.

Browser methods are exploratory; reference R methods require a separately connected local backend. Results are not clinically validated.

Technical evidence: Review the quality-control workflow, reproducible exports and distinction between exploratory browser analyses and reference R methods in the linked source and validation documentation.

PMx Explain

MIPD Engine

Research / educational software

Bayesian forecasting and model-informed precision dosing.

The TDM workflow connects observed concentrations and dosing histories to Bayesian MAP estimation using a library of population PK models.

  • Individualised exposure predictions and future dosing scenarios.
  • Model averaging for compatible models of the same compound, route and administration mode.
  • Experimental ML exposure comparisons when trained artifacts and sampling conditions are available.
  • Patient-data import, export and reports handled within the session.

Technical evidence: The source repository documents model provenance, Bayesian estimation, compatibility constraints for model averaging and qualification tests. These are inspectable software methods, not evidence of clinical outcome benefit.

Not a clinical medical device. Results require critical interpretation; experimental ML estimates do not replace MAP-based dose recommendations.

AI for pharmacometrics

Autonomous Pharmacometrics

Research workflow · under evaluation

Investigating auditable assistance for population PK model building.

This research examines steps in population-model development including structural hypotheses, covariate selection, diagnostics and model comparison. The objective is reproducible, inspectable decisions rather than replacing pharmacometric validation with an AI-generated report.

Evidence and limits: The PAGE 2026 communication describes the research direction. It should not be interpreted as proof that arbitrary models or clinical dosing decisions can be autonomously validated.

Biomedical AI

GRAD

Research / development project

Local-first AI agents for healthcare and biomedical science.

GRAD investigates an offline-first, tool-mediated architecture built around specialist agent delegation and auditable workflows.

The aim is to support scientific and healthcare work while keeping execution local and actions inspectable. This is a research and development project, not a validated clinical product.
