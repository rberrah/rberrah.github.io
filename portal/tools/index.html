MIPD Engine

Research / educational software

Bayesian forecasting and model-informed precision dosing.

The TDM workflow connects observed concentrations and dosing histories to Bayesian MAP estimation using a library of population PK models.

  • Individualised exposure predictions and future dosing scenarios.
  • Model averaging for compatible models of the same compound, route and administration mode.
  • Experimental ML exposure comparisons when trained artifacts and sampling conditions are available.
  • Patient-data import, export and reports handled within the session.

Not a clinical medical device. Results require critical interpretation; experimental ML estimates do not replace MAP-based dose recommendations.

TacDDI

Research project

Precision dosing under complex drug-drug interactions.

MissedDose

Model-based guidance for missed and delayed oral doses.

Biomedical AI

GRAD

Research / development project

Local-first AI agents for healthcare and biomedical science.

GRAD investigates an offline-first, tool-mediated architecture built around specialist agent delegation and auditable workflows.

The aim is to support scientific and healthcare work while keeping execution local and actions inspectable. This is a research and development project, not a validated clinical product.
