Citing a chapter or resource

Use the author, the title as displayed, the last revision date, the resource name and its canonical URL. When citing software, include the version or commit used for your analysis.

Berrah, R. (Year, Month Day). Title of chapter.
Pharmacometrics Explain. Canonical chapter URL.

The original research papers listed in a chapter should be cited separately when their findings or methods support your work. See Publications & Talks for my research output.
