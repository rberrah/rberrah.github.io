Academic curriculum vitae · November 2026

Racym Berrah, PharmD, MSc

Pharmacist and PhD candidate in pharmacology — clinical pharmacometrics, precision dosing and computational pharmacology.

Professional profile

I am a pharmacist with postgraduate training in pharmacokinetics/pharmacodynamics (PK/PD) and a PhD candidate at INSERM UMR 1248 “Pharmacology & Transplantation”, University of Limoges (since November 2025).

I combine population pharmacokinetics, model-informed precision dosing, therapeutic drug monitoring, mechanistic pharmacology and artificial intelligence to develop clinically interpretable quantitative models, particularly for transplantation and anti-infective therapy.

Research interests: population PK/PD, Bayesian forecasting, drug–drug interactions, pharmacogenomics, multi-omics, mechanistic and grey-box modelling, and trustworthy AI.

Education and experience

  • 2025–present — PhD in pharmacology, University of Limoges / INSERM UMR 1248. Thesis on hybrid mechanistic and AI-based approaches to pharmacological digital twins; supervisor: Prof. Jean-Baptiste Woillard.
  • January–October 2025 — Data scientist and pharmacometrician, INSERM UMR 1248, Limoges: population PK, precision dosing and analysis of clinical/pharmacogenomic data.
  • 2024 — PharmD and MSc in PK/PD modelling, Université Paris Cité. Pharmacy thesis defended November 2024.
  • February–August 2024 — Clinical pharmacokinetics research internship, Servier: tumour-growth inhibition and time-to-event modelling in oncology.
  • May–September 2023 — Research internship, INSERM IAME UMR 1137: population PK and clindamycin–rifampicin drug interactions.
  • March–August 2022 — Hospital clinical training, Cochin Hospital (AP-HP), clinical pharmacology and intensive care: therapeutic drug monitoring and PK modelling.
