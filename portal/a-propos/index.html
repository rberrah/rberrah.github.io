About

Racym Berrah, PharmD

Pharmacist and PhD candidate in Pharmacology.

Research

I work at INSERM UMR 1248 “Pharmacology & Transplantation”, University of Limoges / Limoges University Hospital, France.

My work focuses on combining mechanistic pharmacology, pharmacometrics and artificial intelligence to build interpretable and clinically useful quantitative models.

Research interests

  • Population pharmacokinetics and pharmacodynamics.
  • Model-informed precision dosing and Bayesian forecasting.
  • AI-assisted, mechanistic and grey-box pharmacometric modelling.
  • Trustworthy AI for biomedical applications.

Current focus

My PhD explores the integration of pharmacometrics, mechanistic modelling, artificial intelligence and digital-twin concepts in quantitative pharmacology.

Open science & software

I also develop open-source, interactive tools for pharmacometrics, quantitative pharmacology and teaching.
